## Users

- **The owner.** One long-horizon investor in Malaysia who holds crypto, US stocks and ETFs, and Bursa Malaysia stocks. Reads the daily briefing on a phone in Telegram, before 08:00 Asia/Kuala_Lumpur.
- **Showcase visitors.** People who open the public site to understand how the briefing is made: developers, investors curious about the method, and anyone the owner sends the link to *(inferred)*. Most visits are on phones.

## Product Purpose

Every morning the service collects public market data, verifies it, computes indicators, candidate setups, holding reviews and portfolio state in Python, and sends one concise briefing. Most days the right answer is "nothing new". Success is a briefing that arrives every day and that the owner can trust without checking the numbers elsewhere.

The showcase explains that method with a working sample briefing generated by the real pipeline from synthetic data.

## Positioning

Every number in the briefing is computed by deterministic Python and carries its source, session date and quality status. The optional AI narrative can reference numbers only through placeholders that Python fills in. No strategy produces a setup until a backtest report exists for that exact version.

## Operating Context

- The briefing arrives in a private Telegram chat. Commands such as `/why SPY` and `/held` answer in the same chat.
- Runs on GitHub Actions, a Vercel function and managed PostgreSQL. There is no server.
- The showcase is a static site with no connection to the database, Telegram or GitHub. Its data comes from `lens demo export`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Research and decision support only. It never places trades, connects to a broker or gives personalised financial advice. Every briefing and the site say so.
- Analysis uses only completed daily sessions. No intraday alerts.
- Silence is valid. Zero setups is a normal day, and the briefing never pads.
- Single owner. Multi-user is a non-goal.
- The showcase must render its content without JavaScript, and motion must be pausable.
