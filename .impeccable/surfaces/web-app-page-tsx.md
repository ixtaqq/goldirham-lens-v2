# Showcase home

Scope: the one-page public showcase in `web/`. Visitor mode: Experience. The sample briefing leads.

Audience: developers and investors curious how the briefing is made, mostly on phones. Job: understand the method and trust that the numbers come from code. Action: read the sample briefing across three demo days, then the method. Proof: `web/data/demo.json`, produced by the real pipeline over a synthetic market and labelled DEMO DATA. Constraints: content renders without JavaScript; motion is pausable and gentler under reduced motion; no live-looking status; claims stay literal.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One research ledger at night. A briefing made by the real pipeline sits on ruled panels over a field of price traces, and each figure keeps its session date and source. It refuses the fintech landing page of gradient hero, phone mockup, feature cards and testimonials.

OWN-WORLD: Carbon ground, graphite panels, paper text, fog rules and traces. Mint only where a check passed, loss only where one failed. Barlow Condensed headlines, Geist prose, Geist Mono for every figure. 1px rules, square corners, no shadows or glass.

STORY: The visitor watches a trace pass the verification frame and turn mint, reads the actual Telegram render for a quiet day, a setup day and a degraded day, follows one setup being built and one disputed bar withheld, then reads the rules and an honest backtest.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Headline across seven columns at display size, lede and two actions beneath it. The verification frame sits right of centre where traces cross. A demo ticker runs under the header. The day ledger starts at the bottom edge and leads into the briefing.

FORM: pinned by the owner's brand brief (ruled ledger); no concept roll, seed key none.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
